## Next test from saved references

A review of 12 creator references found several explainers that put a demonstration or result on screen while introducing the premise. One visual joke held the setup until a reveal, and one campaign explainer spent several beats setting up its claim. The collection mostly covers clipping tools and creator businesses. The [convertible sequence above](#gta-series-videos-brings-a-gameplay-detail-into-the-portrait-crop) gives the gameplay comparison: the crop keeps the roof visible from activation to the completed movement. These observations motivate an experiment. Viewer response is still unknown.

1. **Scout.** Find a Twitch scene where one visible interaction raises a question and a later frame answers it. Capture the lead-in to the action and its complete result. Pass if the action or result stays unreadable in portrait.
2. **Editor.** Compare two cuts of the same scene. Start one on the object or action with a short caption about what the player is trying to do. Start the other on the creator's reaction or a wider setup. Cut both to the same body at the same time and hold the runtime, grade, sound after the opening, and platform copy constant. Use cleared game sound, creator reaction, or narration at the turn if it helps the viewer recognize what just happened.
3. **Reviewer.** At phone size, ask a fresh viewer what they expect to happen after the opening and what changed by the payoff. Inspect both exports at those beats and verify that the body matches after the opening. Play the sound through the turn and ending. Check names against what appears on screen when speech recognition is uncertain.
4. **Experts and analyst.** Record the treatment selected and why. Test the other opening on an independent scene of similar subject and length. Use YouTube's Stayed to watch and Instagram's opening retention when visible. Record the count and post age alongside the metric. Treat average watch time as a fallback for overall viewing, with the opening effect left unknown. Keep campaign and earnings claims out of performance conclusions unless separately verified.
