# YouTube and Instagram experts

The project-scoped Codex roles are [`youtube_expert`](../.codex/agents/youtube-expert.toml) and [`instagram_expert`](../.codex/agents/instagram-expert.toml). Ask for them by name, in parallel, when briefing a batch or reading back its results. Give both the same scene and intended audiences. Give the decision owner their two separate opinions before the edit. The [production plan](production-workflow-plan.md) governs independent review and publishing. ## Research checked November 19, 2026

Platform eligibility changes. Reopen program terms and read the owner's account-specific status when a recommendation depends on eligibility. Treat an inaccessible page or missing account readback as unverified. A sponsor's approval, a valid upload, and platform monetization are separate outcomes.

### YouTube Shorts

- **Partner Program.** The expanded Shorts route in eligible countries, including India, requires 500 subscribers, three valid public uploads in 90 days, and 3 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days for earlier access to eligible fan funding and Shopping. The Shorts ad and Premium revenue route requires 1,000 subscribers and 10 million qualified public Shorts views in 90 days, or the separate long-form watch-hour route. YouTube reviews the channel and the owner must accept the Shorts Monetization Module for eligible Shorts revenue. YouTube also announces a separate monthly revenue qualification based on 10 million qualified Shorts views over a 90-day period beginning February 1, 2027. Recheck that future rule and the timing of participation in Studio → Earn before making a revenue estimate. [Expanded YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240) · [Shorts monetization](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)
- **Rights and originality.** Third-party gameplay and stream audio need the appropriate rights for the intended use. A streamer or campaign's permission can cover its own materials. It does not establish rights held by the game publisher or a music rightsholder. YouTube's channel-wide reused-content policy evaluates the viewer-facing original contribution separately from copyright permission. Captions, reframing and an attribution line alone give little evidence of meaningful original value. For After the Spike, give the scene an authored point of view, for example a time-saving explanation of how a mechanic works, a comparison with a visible outcome, or a distinctly told story. Account for the game license and the advertiser-friendly gaming rules before revenue assumptions. [Reused content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392) · [Game and software content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/138161) · [Gaming and monetization](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10291745)
- **Length and results.** YouTube accepts square or vertical Shorts up to three minutes under its current classification rules. An active copyright claim blocks a Short over one minute. These are format and rights rules. Choose runtime around the payoff and test shorter cuts within similar scenes. Use the feed's Stayed to watch and engaged views for the opening. Look at average view duration and percentage viewed for the viewing that follows. Report public views with their own label, since starts and replays count differently from the qualified/engaged metrics used for YPP and earnings. Retention highlights can be absent even where per-video data exists. [Shorts length](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877) · [Shorts analytics](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281) · [Retention](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415)

### Instagram Reels

- **Revenue surfaces.** Meta describes fan-paid Gifts, recurring creator Subscriptions, and branded collaboration via Creator Marketplace and partnership ads. Meta announced Marketplace expansion to India. These routes have different eligibility requirements. Start in the owner's Professional Dashboard to confirm account type, region, current availability, policy status, and payout setup for the exact feature. The Gifts launch announcement is historical. Its follower threshold should not stand in for the current in-app requirements. Likewise, Meta's historical invite-only bonus test is not evidence of an active offer for After the Spike. No per-view earnings or account eligibility was verified here. [Meta earning products](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/11/giving-creators-more-ways-to-earn-money-on-facebook-and-instagram/) · [Creator Marketplace](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/creator-marketplace-for-brands-and-creators-to-collaborate-on-instagram/) · [Best Practices in the Professional Dashboard](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/)
- **Recommendations and rights.** Meta described replacing detected identical reposts with originals in Instagram recommendations and allowing for substantive editorial changes in that matching policy. Review the current Account Status and policies for the specific post and account. Recommend an original narrative, useful context, or a revealing comparison rather than surface branding. Reuse permission and an editable soundtrack are separate from any payout/recommendation ruling. Meta's November 2026 India update also describes limits for mature content in recommendations. Take extra care with footage whose primary payoff is graphic content. [Meta's original-content announcement](https://about.fb.com/ltam/news/2024/05/ayudando-a-los-creadores-a-encontrar-nuevas-audiencias/) · [India recommendations](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/11/an-update-on-the-content-we-recommend-on-instagram-in-india/) · [Content Monetization Policies](https://help.instagram.com/2635536099905516)
- **Length and results.** Use account-specific Best Practices, current composer limits, and the scene's necessary story beats. Compare competing openings with the remaining cut and runtime held as stable as possible. Capture displayed views, accounts reached, average watch time, and shares. If an opening skip or retention metric is available, preserve its actual label. Average watch time alone cannot tell whether viewers stayed until a particular beat. If using Trial Reels, only compare with other trials or explicitly distinguish the different audience and release time. Meta describes an initial insights read around 24 hours for trials. [Best Practices](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/) · [Watch time](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/04/instagram-reels-trending-audio-and-gifts-updates/) · [Edits insights](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/04/introducing-edits-streamlined-video-creation-app/) · [Trial Reels](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/)

Direct fetches of Instagram's Help Center monetization pages and current creator originality pages were throttled during research. Use reachable Meta documentation plus the owner dashboard for current thresholds, account state and rule changes.

## Reverse-engineer the decision

Use the creator's own public posts or first-person descriptions. Start with several comparable posts on that creator's channel, including ordinary examples as a comparison. Record URL, publication age when available, sample timestamp, premise, what the first frame promises, first clear payoff, duration, visible packaging and sound only when inspected. Extract a portable technique. Build a fresh premise, original copy and assets cleared for After the Spike. Public counts and samples cannot reveal a creator's private retention, earnings or causal effect.

## Expert handoff

Return one dated brief per platform. Include these entries in the local round record.

| Recipient | What the expert hands over |
| --- | --- |
| Scout | One specific scene signal and one reason to pass. Identify the creator example or previous round that motivates it. |
| Editor and reviewer | A recommended opening, one viable alternative, proposed runtime or range with the retained beats, the scene's timecoded visual payoff, and what the editor adds. Say what a fresh viewer should understand at phone size. |
| Publisher | Recommended title or caption angle and cover choice. Flag eligibility, credit, claim, or rights questions to resolve before assuming monetization. |
| Analyst and next scout | One prediction and a directional result that would change the recommendation. Name the primary metric and denominator, comparison group, confounders and a fallback if the metric is absent. |

At release, use the queue receipt for the actual public timestamp. At 24 hours, 72 hours and seven days, use the [round log](studio-rounds.md) to record verified owner readbacks and the [timing guide](short-form-experiments.md) to choose an honest comparison. Provide a provisional opinion when evidence is sparse. Keep confirmed policy, observed example, and editorial hypothesis separately labeled.

Example handoff for a 13-second in-game phone scene: advise a scout to clip the first readable dating profile through the last joke, decline the candidate when a phone crop still cannot make the joke readable, and test a readable-phone opening against the bar-wide opening with an otherwise identical cut. Lead the Short title with the discovery and the Reel caption with the oddity viewers would send. Ask the Shorts analyst for Stayed to watch and the Reels analyst for displayed opening retention, using average watch time as a labeled fallback only. Inspect retention through the last joke if available. If the opening already holds attention but the last card loses it across comparable posts, trim the setup before changing the premise. The [Lacy field round](studio-rounds.md#lacys-dating-profiles) records the visual review. It is not a causal result.
