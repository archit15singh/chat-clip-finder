| 5 | Platform and audience conversion | Use eligible posts on Reels and Shorts where the brief permits cross-posting. Compare per-platform reach and qualified traffic at the same post age. | Approved earnings, new followers, and qualified inquiries |
| 6 | Editing throughput | Batch captions and export presets after the format earns. Audit rejected cues and messages-per-bucket against manual labels before increasing volume. | Approved clips per hour, rejection rate, and contribution per hour |

## Round log

- **Round 1 · Multiple channels and a render.** A deterministic replay produced two distinct cues from simultaneous chat spikes. FFmpeg decoded a 30.000-second 1080 × 1920 H.264/AAC draft. The offline VOD baseline stayed unchanged across all four cases.
- **Round 2 · Timing split.** Replayed the four historical GTA V cases at 12, 18, and 24 seconds of lead-in, holding the full window at 30 seconds. Each produced ten complete windows and caught one of three marked anchors. The 12- and 18-second splits both covered one of ten labeled intervals. The 24-second split lost that interval. Keep 18 seconds as the default. Use new GTA VI labels to test scene-aware trimming. Reproduce with .improvement/rounds/2026-11-18-boundaries.py and inspect its JSON output.
