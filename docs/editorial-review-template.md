# Clip review

Copy this into the local round folder. Use one sheet per revision. Keep the raw source, selected cuts, media evidence, and review local.

## Candidate

- Creator and original URL
- Source clock and selected range
- Chat cue and replay evidence, when applicable
- Reuse permission for each destination, with scope and source
- What the viewer notices first
- Setup, turn, and reaction

Name the one deliberate change. For a reusable template, try it on replacement media and check that framing, edit points, and audio still work.

## Independent review

- Editor and reviewer
- Selected export SHA-256, report path, and inspected timestamps
- Does the opening make sense at phone size without sound?
- What improved in the revision? Give a visible or audible example.
- Sound mode, rationale, and rights evidence. Listen through the exact export with sound on, then check the opening on mute. Record the sound cues, sync, mix, music clearance, and any disclosure. For a trackless export, verify the intentional silence exception.
- Why is this worth sharing? Describe the scene in your own words.
- YouTube account, title, and description
- Instagram account and caption
- Creator credit, disclosures, and rights check
- One experiment and the first measurement age
- Approve, revise, or escalate an unresolved exception
